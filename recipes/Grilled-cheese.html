Can't go wrong with a grilled cheest toasty see below

Ingredients

  • Cheese (personal preference i.e Mozarella, cheddar vintage cheddar)
  • Bread (White or wholemeal)
  • Salted butter

Instructions

  1. Grate cheese If not using store-bought pre-sliced cheese, grate it using a standard box grater. Pre-shredded cheese has anti-caking agents that prevent proper melting.
  2. Butter both sides of each bread Yes, all four sides! This helps with crispiness and flavor.
  3. Head start fry Place the bread in a preheated heavy-based skillet or non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Lightly toast for 1 minute to enhance flavor and help the cheese melt faster.
  4. Flip bread & cheese it! Turn one slice over, pile cheese on top, then place the other slice with its toasted side against the cheese.
  5. 3 minutes Press lightly and cook for 3 minutes until the underside is golden brown. If browning too quickly, lower the heat.
  6. Turn, 3 minutes Carefully flip and cook for another 3 minutes until golden and crisp. Check to ensure the cheese has melted.
